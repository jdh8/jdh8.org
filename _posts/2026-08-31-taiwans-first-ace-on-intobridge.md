I used to boycott [IntoBridge][ib] because Taiwan was not in its country
selector.  A country dropdown looks trivial until your country is not in it.
So I closed the tab and played bridge elsewhere.

[ib]: https://intobridge.com/

Recently I gave it another try, and there it was: Taiwan, flag and all.  So I
stayed.  Two weeks and 549 ranked games later, I became Taiwan's first Ace,
ranked #1 in Taiwan and #338 in the world.

![My IntoBridge dashboard, showing rank #338, the Taiwanese flag, and a
month-long graph: flat at 7&#x2666; until August 10, then a climb to Ace in two
weeks]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/intobridge-ace.png)

Naturally I wanted to know *when* Taiwan was added.  I found no announcement,
so I asked Codex to investigate.  I even disclosed my ulterior motive up
front:

> Humans are not completely rational and would waste tokens on showing off.

The investigation went considerably further than the brag required.  Codex
compared archived copies of the country list in the site's JavaScript.  The
old list had 195 options; the current one has 197.  The only additions are
Taiwan and Hong Kong.  Their flag files ([tw.svg], [hk.svg]) carry exactly
the same server timestamp, 25 February 2026 15:34:12 UTC, while most other
flags date back to 2022.  That is not an official release date, but it
strongly suggests both were added together that day.

[tw.svg]: https://play.intobridge.com/assets/svg/flags/tw.svg
[hk.svg]: https://play.intobridge.com/assets/svg/flags/hk.svg

![The Taiwan leaderboard on IntoBridge, with jdh8 at
#1]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/intobridge-taiwan-leaderboard.png)

Was it rational to spend tokens on this?  No.  But the flag is there, my name
sits on top of it, and now the date is on the record.  Tokens well spent.
🇹🇼♣️
